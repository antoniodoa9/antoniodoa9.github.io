About me

I'm a student of Health Engineering at the University of Málaga. I've completed the Biomedical Engineering track and I'm now finishing the Bioinformatics track too, so my training covers both sides: biomechanics, medical instrumentation and physiological modelling on one hand, and genomics, data mining and machine learning on the other.

What I enjoy most is turning clinical and biological data into something a doctor could actually trust. That is why I care about the method as much as the result: repeated cross-validation instead of a single split, a simple baseline to compare against, and code that anyone can run again from the repository.

Before engineering there was music. I spent twelve years at the conservatory, and the habit of daily practice and patience still shapes how I approach technical work.

Oncology notes classification

Clinical NLP on 239 real Spanish oncology notes to detect patients with more than one neoplasm. The best model raises the AUC from 0.733 to 0.827 and, with a clinical threshold, finds 91 % of those patients.

  • spaCy
  • scikit-learn
  • Nested CV

Antibiotics and breast cancer response

Data mining on a clinical cohort: patients without antibiotics reached complete response in 48.1 % of cases against 38 % (p = 0.011). The final model runs as BioCalc, a Shiny app.

  • R
  • Quarto
  • Shiny
  • Multiple imputation

RNA-seq of Arabidopsis: Ler vs C24

A full RNA-seq workflow, from raw reads to 28 differentially expressed genes: photosynthesis is more active in Ler and defence response in C24.

PK-PD model of propofol anaesthesia

A three-compartment model with effect site and Hill equation that simulates how deep each patient's anaesthesia goes, and why one fixed dose does not fit everyone.
